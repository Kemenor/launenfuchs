# Launenfuchs 🦊

A **mood journal** whose statistics are yours. Rate the day, tap what you did, add a note
if you like — and see how your weeks actually went, on your phone, with no subscription.

> Built because the app I used put every chart behind a paywall the week I needed them
> for a therapy session — and a mood journal is a small database with a nice entry screen.

## The idea

- ⏱️ **Ten seconds a day.** One tap for the mood, a few for what you did, done. The
  reminder opens straight into today.
- 🗂️ **Categories that fit how you log.** *Pick one per day* (worked normally / less /
  day off — as chips or a slider, with a default) or *any number* (walk, gym).
- 🗓️ **Periods.** Mark *Holiday*, *Moving flat*, *New job* — and see your mood
  phase by phase. Often the clearest view there is.
- 📊 **All stats, free.** Day by day, by period, mood alongside what you logged, weekdays, notes.
  Honest about small numbers; never pretends a pattern is a cause.
- 📄 **A report to take along.** Pick a range, export a PDF for your therapist — notes
  only if you say so.
- 📥 **Bring your Daylio history — your way.** Reads the free Daylio backup and lets you
  rename, merge or drop categories and options on the way in.
- 🎨 **Calm by design.** No red for bad days, no streak guilt, no ads. Dark + light, four
  languages, dyslexia- and low-vision-friendly fonts.

## Privacy

Fully on-device. No accounts, no analytics, no server. Nothing leaves the phone unless you
export it.
