Privacy Policy

Launenfuchs · last updated 15 October 2026

EN · DE · FR · IT

The short version: Launenfuchs has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your mood journal lives in a database on your phone. The app never talks to a server — we don’t run one and we never see your data.

What the app stores

Your entries (mood, what you logged, notes), categories and options, periods and settings — in a local SQLite database inside the app’s private storage on your device. Reminders are computed on the device and handed to your phone’s notification system.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, by design. Launenfuchs makes no network requests. Data leaves the device only when you export it: a backup ZIP, or a report (HTML or PDF) — saved wherever you choose. Notes are left out of reports unless you switch them on.

Importing from Daylio

When you import a Daylio backup, the file is read on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and the Daylio file is not changed.

Permissions

Notifications, for the optional daily reminder. Biometrics / screen lock, for the optional app lock — your phone checks your fingerprint, face or PIN; the app only learns “unlocked” or “not unlocked” and never sees the biometric data.

Deleting your data

Delete single days in the journal, or a category “for good” in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything. There is no account to close and nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Children

Launenfuchs collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above moves and the change is described here. The app will not start collecting data without a new version and a new policy.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Kemenor/launenfuchs.
