Launenfuchs

A mood journal whose statistics are yours. Rate the day, tap what you did — and see how your weeks actually went.

The idea

  • Ten seconds a day. One tap for the mood, a few for what you did.
  • Categories that fit how you log — one per day (as chips or a slider, with a default) or any number.
  • Periods. Mark a holiday, a move, a new job — and see your mood phase by phase.
  • All stats, free — honest about small numbers, never pretending a pattern is a cause.
  • A report to take along — HTML or PDF for your therapist, notes only if you say so.
  • Bring your Daylio history from its free backup file.
  • Calm by design — no red for bad days, no streak guilt, no ads, no subscription.

Privacy

Fully on-device. No accounts, no analytics, no server. Privacy policy.

Source

Open source (Apache-2.0) at github.com/Kemenor/launenfuchs. Part of the fuchs family.
